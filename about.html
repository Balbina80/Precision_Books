About Precision Books

Personal accounting support you can trust.

Precision Books was created to provide straightforward, professional accounting services for small businesses, landlords and self-employed individuals.

My Story

Why I Started Precision Books

After more than 9 years working in accounting and business administration, I realised that many business owners were looking for something simple: clear advice, reliable support and someone they could trust.

That's why I created Precision Books — to offer a personal approach to accounting, without unnecessary jargon or complexity.

Today I work with self-employed individuals, landlords and limited companies, helping them stay compliant, organised and confident about their finances.
