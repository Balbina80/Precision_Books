Can I Claim My Home Office Expenses?

A simple guide for UK business owners, sole traders and limited company directors.

Published by Precision Books

← Back to Blog

If you run your business from home, you may be able to claim certain household costs as a business expense and reduce your tax bill. However, many business owners are unsure what they can claim and how to do it correctly.

This guide explains the basics of home office expenses for UK self-employed individuals and limited company directors.

What Are Home Office Expenses?

Home office expenses are costs associated with using part of your home for business purposes.

You may be able to claim a proportion of expenses such as:

  • Electricity and gas
  • Water bills
  • Council Tax
  • Mortgage interest, but not capital repayments
  • Rent
  • Broadband and telephone costs
  • Home insurance

The amount you can claim depends on how much of your home is used for business and how often it is used.

Self-Employed: Simplified Expenses

If you are self-employed, HMRC offers a simplified method known as simplified expenses.

Instead of calculating actual household costs, you can claim a flat rate based on the number of hours you work from home each month.

  • 25 to 50 hours per month: £10 per month
  • 51 to 100 hours per month: £18 per month
  • 101 hours or more per month: £26 per month

This option is often easier for small businesses and sole traders because it keeps the calculation simple.

Self-Employed: Actual Cost Method

Alternatively, you can calculate the actual proportion of household expenses relating to your business.

For example, if you use one room as an office, your home has five rooms, and the office is used for business 50% of the time, you may be able to claim an appropriate proportion of qualifying household costs.

Good record keeping is essential if you choose this method.

Limited Company Directors

If you operate through a limited company, the rules are different.

Many directors work from home and may be able to:

  • Claim a small HMRC-approved home working allowance through the company
  • Reimburse actual business expenses where appropriate
  • Use a formal rental agreement in certain circumstances

The most suitable option depends on your individual situation and should be discussed with your accountant.

Can I Claim Broadband?

If you already had broadband before starting your business, you can usually only claim the business-use portion.

If the broadband contract is taken out specifically for business use, a larger proportion may be allowable.

It is important to separate personal and business use where possible.

Can I Claim My Mobile Phone?

Self-employed individuals may claim the business-use percentage of mobile phone costs.

Limited companies can often provide a company mobile phone with potential tax advantages, provided HMRC rules are followed.

What Records Should I Keep?

To support any claims, keep records such as:

  • Utility bills
  • Broadband bills
  • Phone bills
  • Rent statements
  • Mortgage interest statements
  • Home insurance documents

Maintaining accurate records will make your tax return easier and help if HMRC requests evidence.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many business owners make mistakes such as:

  • Claiming personal expenses as business expenses
  • Claiming 100% of household costs without justification
  • Failing to keep supporting records
  • Using estimates that cannot be explained

Making incorrect claims could result in HMRC enquiries and additional tax liabilities.

Final Thoughts

Working from home can provide valuable tax relief, but it is important to claim expenses correctly.

The rules vary depending on whether you are self-employed or operate through a limited company. Taking professional advice can help ensure you maximise legitimate tax deductions while remaining compliant with HMRC requirements.
