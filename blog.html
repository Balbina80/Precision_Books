April 2026 Tax Updates

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax: What Changed in April 2026?

From April 2026, Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax began to apply to self-employed individuals and landlords with qualifying income above HMRC thresholds.

Who is affected?

MTD for Income Tax currently applies to individuals with annual gross income of more than £50,000 from self-employment, property income, or a combination of both.

What does this mean?

  • Digital record keeping is required.
  • Compatible accounting software must be used.
  • Quarterly updates must be submitted to HMRC.
  • An End of Period Statement (EOPS) is required.
  • A Final Declaration replaces the traditional Self Assessment process.

Who will be affected next?

From April 2027, MTD for Income Tax is expected to apply to individuals with qualifying income above £30,000.

How can Precision Books help?

If you are self-employed or receive rental income, Precision Books can help you prepare for Making Tax Digital, choose suitable software and ensure you remain compliant with HMRC requirements.
